# Financial Performance Analysis: Funding Circle vs LendInvest (2022–2024)

## Project Overview
A comprehensive financial and operational analysis of two UK fintech firms — **Funding Circle (FCH)** and **LendInvest (LINV)** — from 2022 to 2024. The project evaluates profitability, efficiency, cost management, liquidity, and operational health, and translates metrics into **strategic business insights**.

## Objectives
- Benchmark financial and operational performance of FCH vs LINV  
- Identify growth drivers, cost inefficiencies, and liquidity gaps  
- Quantify business impact through scenario modeling  

## Data & Tools
- **Python**: Data extraction, cleaning, and integration  
- **SQL**: Metric computation, scenario modeling, analytical depth  
- **Power BI**: Executive dashboards for data-driven decision-making

### Operating Efficiency & Cost Management
- **FCH**: Stable efficiency (~0.50–0.55), costs exceeded revenue until 2024  
- **LINV**: Efficiency improved, cost spike in 2024 impacted profits  
**Impact:** Highlights cost optimization opportunities  

### Profitability & Revenue
- **FCH**: Turned around from −29% (2023) to +5% (2024)  
- **LINV**: Peaked +14% (2023) but dropped to −30% (2024)  
**Impact:** Links profitability trends to market perception and strategic resilience  

### Liquidity & Cash Runway
- **FCH**: >30-month runway, stable operations  
- **LINV**: Volatile runway (3–22 months), potential liquidity risk  
**Impact:** Evaluates capital efficiency and investment flexibility  

### Scenario Modeling
- Revenue sensitivity, efficiency savings, and gross margin optimization  
- Example: Matching LINV’s efficiency could have saved FCH ~£32M  
**Impact:** Quantifies potential gains and strategic decisions  

## Dashboard & Storytelling
- Power BI dashboards highlight **Revenue & Profitability, Cash Flow & Efficiency, CapEx & Liquidity**  
- Each visualization includes **concise insights** for quick executive interpretation
